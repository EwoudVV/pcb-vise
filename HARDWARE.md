## optional ESC attachment

the two printed adapters need eight M3 x 25 screws, four extra M3 nuts and four M3 washers. the nuts already fitted in the jaws are reused. those extra fasteners cost $1.12 before shipping and tax

use the longer screws with the ESC adapters. the standard edge grips use M3 x 20 screws

## check before ordering

use the nut sizes below. taller nuts, including Nylocs, won't fit the pockets

| Nut | Across flats | Height | Pocket across flats |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| M3 | 5.5 mm | 2.4 mm | 5.8 mm |
| M4 | 7 mm | 3.2 mm | 7.3 mm |
| M5 | 8 mm | 4 mm | 8.3 mm |
| M6 | 10 mm | 5 mm | 10.3 mm |

the M5 pockets are only 4.4 mm deep, so a 4.7 mm tall nut won't work. check the actual hardware with the fit samples before printing all the parts. screw lengths are measured from under the head

## ballast

order four supplied-cut pieces of 3/4 x 1 1/2 inch 1018 steel bar, each 2.362 inches long (about 60 mm). the [custom-cut quote](https://www.onlinemetals.com/en/buy/carbon-steel/0-75-x-1-5-carbon-steel-rectangle-bar-1018-cold-finish/pid/9720) is $5.98 each

the supplier allows +1/8 inch and no undersize on the cut length. the liners now have 64 mm inside length and the foot pockets are 67 mm long, so pieces up to 63.17 mm fit. file any sharp burrs before fitting them. no sawing is planned
