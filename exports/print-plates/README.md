# print plates

for my P1S with a 0.4 mm hardened-steel nozzle and textured PEI plate. these are editable Bambu Studio projects. start with the fit samples during build week before printing this whole set

| Plate | Material | Time estimate | Filament |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| [01-feet](01-feet.3mf) | PETG-CF | 9h 40m | 298 g |
| [02-uprights](02-uprights.3mf) | PETG-CF | 3h 46m | 111 g |
| [03-bars-and-adapters](03-bars-and-adapters.3mf) | PETG-CF | 2h 41m | 76 g |
| [04-jaws-and-pivots](04-jaws-and-pivots.3mf) | PETG-CF | 7h 22m | 215 g |
| [05-lids](05-lids.3mf) | PETG-CF | 1h 27m | 48 g |
| [06-tpu](06-tpu.3mf) | TPU for AMS | 2h 47m | 115 g |

about 749 g of PETG-CF and 115 g of TPU for the full set, including the alternative edge grips. these are slicer estimates, with no allowance for failed prints or extra fit samples

the rigid parts use 0.20 mm layers, four walls and 30% gyroid infill. the TPU parts use three walls and 15% infill. the thin rings and liner floors come out solid

only the feet have supports enabled. clean them out of the side slots and nut pockets before assembly. the right-hand base bars are flipped so the lap joints print flat

open a project, check the material and plate, then slice again before printing. nothing has been printed yet
